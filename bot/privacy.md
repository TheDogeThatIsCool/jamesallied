# James Allied Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **James Allied Bot** ("the Bot"), operated by **James Allied** ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Bot.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We May Collect

The Bot may process limited information from Discord that is necessary to provide its features.

Depending on how you use the Bot, this may include:

### Discord Information

* Discord User ID
* Discord Server/Guild ID
* Discord Channel ID
* Discord Role ID
* Discord username or display name
* Discord server configuration information
* Bot command usage
* Permissions and configuration settings relevant to Bot functionality

We only intend to process information that is reasonably necessary for the Bot's operation.

### Information You Provide

Some Bot commands may allow you to voluntarily provide information.

For example, you may provide information when:

* Using a command.
* Configuring the Bot.
* Submitting a support request.
* Reporting an issue.
* Providing feedback.

Please avoid providing passwords, authentication tokens, financial information, or other highly sensitive information through Bot commands.

## 2. Message Content

The Bot may receive message content through Discord when required for a particular feature or command.

**The Bot does not intentionally store the contents of ordinary Discord messages unless a specific feature requires the information to be stored.**

If a feature requires message content to be processed or stored, the relevant feature should make that purpose reasonably clear.

Server administrators should review the Bot's permissions and configuration before enabling features that process message content.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

* Provide Bot commands and features.
* Maintain server-specific settings.
* Respond to commands.
* Authenticate or identify users within Discord.
* Prevent spam and abuse.
* Detect and investigate misuse.
* Troubleshoot technical problems.
* Maintain and improve the Bot.
* Maintain security and reliability.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use information for purposes unrelated to the operation, security, or improvement of the Bot unless otherwise disclosed.

## 4. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases, configuration files, logs, or other systems used to operate the Bot.

The exact amount and type of information stored may vary depending on the features enabled by a server.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

Some information may be retained longer when necessary for:

* Security and abuse prevention.
* Debugging and troubleshooting.
* Legal obligations.
* Maintaining server configurations.
* Resolving disputes or support requests.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, it may be deleted or anonymized.

## 6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with third parties only when reasonably necessary to operate the Bot or when required by law.

Examples may include:

* Hosting providers.
* Database providers.
* Infrastructure providers.
* Third-party APIs used by Bot features.
* Security or abuse-prevention services.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and terms.

We may also disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to:

* Comply with applicable law.
* Respond to valid legal requests.
* Protect the rights or safety of users.
* Investigate abuse or security incidents.
* Protect the Bot and its infrastructure.

## 7. Discord

James Allied Bot operates through Discord.

When you interact with the Bot, Discord may provide information to the Bot through its APIs and platform functionality.

Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

James Allied is not responsible for Discord's collection, processing, storage, or use of information outside of information processed by the Bot.

The Bot is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to independently collect information from children for purposes unrelated to providing its Discord functionality.

Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and the Bot.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from minors.

## 9. Your Choices

Depending on the information involved, you may be able to request that certain information associated with your use of the Bot be deleted or corrected.

For example, if the Bot stores server configuration or other information associated with a particular server, a server administrator may request its deletion.

Some information may need to be retained when required for security, legal, or legitimate operational purposes.

To make a privacy-related request, contact the James Allied team through the official support channels provided for the Bot.

## 10. Server Administrators

Server administrators control whether the Bot is installed and which permissions it receives.

Administrators should:

* Review the Bot's requested permissions.
* Configure features appropriately.
* Avoid granting unnecessary permissions.
* Inform their server members about relevant Bot functionality where appropriate.

Installing the Bot does not give James Allied unrestricted access to a server's data. The Bot can generally only access information that Discord makes available based on its permissions, intents, APIs, and the features being used.

## 11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the Bot.

However, we cannot guarantee that information transmitted over the Internet or stored electronically will always remain completely secure.

If we become aware of a significant security incident affecting user information, we will take appropriate steps to investigate and respond.

## 12. Third-Party Services

Certain Bot features may depend on third-party services or APIs.

When a feature interacts with a third-party service, information necessary for that feature may be transmitted to that service.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services before using features that depend on them.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the **Last Updated** date at the top of this policy.

Continued use of the Bot after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 14. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the information processed by James Allied Bot, please contact the James Allied team through the official support channels provided for the Bot.
